Effective date: 22 July 2026

This app is designed to be privacy-first. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking frameworks.

Data we process

  • On-device study data: progress, answers, bookmarks, streaks, exam date, daily goals, and similar preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchases: subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed by Apple through StoreKit / App Store. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Optional notifications: if you enable study reminders, iOS schedules local notifications on your device. We do not send push notifications from our servers.
  • Support email: if you email us, we receive the content you send so we can respond.

Data we do not collect

  • No account registration inside the app
  • No analytics or advertising identifiers used by us
  • No sale of personal data

Children

The app is a general education study aid. Parents/guardians should supervise purchases made with Family Sharing or their Apple Account.

Third parties

Apple provides App Store, payment, and restore services under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Your choices

You can reset study progress in Settings, disable reminders, or delete the app to remove on-device data. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
